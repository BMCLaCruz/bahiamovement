Questions

Do I need experience?

No. Your first class is free: arrive 10 minutes early and we'll set you up on the mat.

How much does it cost?

Unlimited monthly plans: $1,500 MXN for one discipline (BJJ all 5 days or Muay Thai all 3), $1,800 MXN for both, and $1,500 MXN for BJJ Kids. Registration: $500 MXN.

Do you have kids classes?

Yes. BJJ Kids, Monday to Thursday, 5:00 – 6:00 p.m.

Are classes taught in English?

Yes. Every class is bilingual, in English and Spanish.

Where are you?

Calamar 12, inside Torre N, in La Cruz de Huanacaxtle: between Bucerías and Punta de Mita on the Riviera Nayarit, north of Puerto Vallarta.
